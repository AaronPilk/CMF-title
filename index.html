How it works

From order to clear to close

Four steps, no guesswork. You always know where your file stands.

01

Send the order

Email your contract or order details to orders@cmftitle.com. One message is all it takes to get started.

02

We search the title

We pull the chain of title, prior policies, liens and easements — and flag anything that needs clearing.

03

Commitment issued

You get a timely, accurate title commitment, plus a plain-English plan for resolving any exceptions we find.

04

Clear to close

We coordinate the closing attorney or notary, satisfy liens, and get everyone to the table on schedule.
